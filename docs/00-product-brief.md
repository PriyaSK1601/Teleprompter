# Product Brief

## Product Summary

This product is a Windows-first desktop teleprompter app for people who need to read prepared text while looking natural on camera. The app provides a floating, always-on-top overlay near the top middle of the screen, close to the webcam, plus a separate editor window for creating and managing scripts.

The MVP should be local-first, discreet, reliable, and fast to operate. A user should be able to paste a script, open the overlay, position it near the webcam, start a short countdown, and control scrolling from any app using global keyboard shortcuts.

## Target Users

- Professionals recording short videos, demos, sales updates, training clips, or async updates.
- Creators recording YouTube, TikTok, LinkedIn, course, or podcast video content.
- Presenters giving webinars, live streams, internal presentations, or screen shares.
- Job seekers practicing interviews or reading structured answers.
- Founders, executives, coaches, educators, and consultants who need polished delivery without memorizing scripts.

## Core Use Cases

- Paste a script and read it during a webcam recording.
- Keep a small prompt near the webcam during Zoom, Teams, Meet, Loom, OBS, or PowerPoint.
- Pause, resume, speed up, slow down, and restart scrolling without leaving the current app.
- Save reusable scripts locally for repeated recordings.
- Adjust text size, line spacing, overlay opacity, and overlay dimensions for readability.
- Use a countdown to get ready before scrolling begins.

## Product Wedge

The wedge is not "another script editor." The wedge is a practical, desktop-wide floating teleprompter that works with the tools people already use: video call apps, recording tools, slide decks, browsers, and screen sharing workflows.

The first product promise should be:

> Put readable words near your webcam and control them without breaking eye contact.

The app wins if it feels trustworthy during real recording sessions. Reliability matters more than feature volume.

## What Makes This Different

- Desktop-wide overlay rather than browser-tab-only prompting.
- Global shortcuts designed for use while another app has focus.
- Local-first scripts and settings with no account or backend required.
- Professional, subtle overlay styling instead of a large presenter window.
- Early testing against real meeting and recording software.
- Explicit attention to capture behavior, privacy, and screen-share limitations.

## What Not To Build Yet

- Chrome extension as the primary product.
- Voice tracking or microphone-based scroll control.
- Cloud sync, accounts, teams, collaboration, or payments.
- AI script writing, rewriting, or summarization.
- Complex teleprompter studio features such as multi-camera routing.
- Advanced analytics or reading-performance dashboards.
- Word-by-word karaoke-style highlighting.
- Marketplace templates or shared public script libraries.

## MVP Product Positioning

The MVP should be positioned as a practical desktop utility, not a full production suite. The right standard is "safe to use in a real call or recording," not "complete for every prompting workflow."
